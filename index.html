1. 我們處理的資料

1. Information We Process

我們不要求使用者註冊帳號，也不會主動要求您提供姓名、電話、Email、精確地址或身分證明文件。

We do not require users to create an account, and we do not directly ask for personal information such as name, phone number, email address, precise address, or identity documents.

為了提供 App 功能、廣告、App 內購買、訂閱、分析、當機回報與遠端設定，部分資料可能會由裝置本地端處理，或由第三方服務依其隱私權政策處理。

To provide app features, advertising, in-app purchases, subscriptions, analytics, crash reporting, and remote configuration, certain data may be processed locally on your device or by third-party services according to their own privacy policies.

（1）本地資料

(1) Local Data

App 可能會在您的裝置本地端儲存功能所需資料，例如使用者設定、播放設定、遊戲設定、轉盤內容、計時器設定、主題偏好、購買狀態、影片清單或錄影相關設定。

這些資料主要用於 App 功能運作。我們不會主動將這些本地資料上傳至我們自己的伺服器。

The apps may store data locally on your device, such as user settings, playback settings, game settings, wheel content, timer settings, theme preferences, purchase status, video lists, or recording-related settings. This data is mainly used for app functionality. We do not actively upload this local data to our own servers.

（2）裝置權限

(2) Device Permissions

部分 App 可能會請求相機、麥克風、相簿或通知權限，用於錄製影片、錄製聲音、匯入或儲存影片、匯出使用者產生的內容，或發送本機提醒通知。

除非您主動使用相關功能，否則 App 不會存取、錄製、儲存或分享您的媒體內容。

Some apps may request camera, microphone, photo library, or notification permissions to record videos, capture audio, import or save videos, export user-generated content, or send local reminder notifications. The apps do not access, record, save, or share your media content unless you actively use the related features.

2. 廣告與追蹤

2. Advertising and Tracking

本服務可能使用第三方廣告服務顯示廣告。廣告可能包含個人化廣告或非個人化廣告。

第三方廣告服務可能會依其隱私權政策處理裝置資訊、裝置識別碼、廣告識別碼、廣告互動資料、產品互動資料、粗略位置、診斷資料或效能資料。

在 iOS 裝置上，若 App 使用追蹤或存取廣告識別碼，會依 Apple App Tracking Transparency（ATT）規範請求使用者授權。您可以在 iOS 系統設定中管理追蹤權限。

The Services may use third-party advertising services to display ads. Ads may include personalized or non-personalized ads. Third-party advertising services may process device information, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, advertising data, product interaction data, approximate location, diagnostics, or performance data according to their privacy policies. On iOS, when an app uses tracking or accesses the advertising identifier, it requests user permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings.

3. Analytics, Crash Reporting, and Remote Configuration

部分 App 可能使用第三方服務提供使用分析、當機回報或遠端設定功能。這些服務可能會處理裝置資訊、使用情形、產品互動、當機紀錄、錯誤紀錄、診斷資料或效能資料，用於改善 App 穩定性、功能與服務品質。

我們不會使用這些資料直接識別您的真實身分。

Some apps may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, or remote configuration. These services may process device information, usage data, product interactions, crash logs, error logs, diagnostics, or performance data to improve app stability, features, and service quality. We do not use this data to directly identify your real-world identity.

4. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

部分 App 提供 App 內購買或自動續訂訂閱。付款、續訂、取消訂閱與購買紀錄由 Apple 透過 App Store 處理。

我們不會收集或儲存您的信用卡號碼、銀行帳戶資料、帳單地址或付款驗證資料。

We do not collect or store your credit card number, bank account information, billing address, or payment authentication details. Such service may process anonymous user identifiers, product IDs, purchase status, subscription status, or transaction validation information to verify purchase eligibility, unlock paid features, restore purchases, or manage subscription status.

本服務可能使用或連結至第三方服務，例如廣告服務、分析與當機回報服務、購買管理服務、App Store，或使用者主動開啟的外部網站與服務。第三方服務會依其各自的隱私權政策處理資料。我們會盡合理努力選擇提供適當資料保護措施的第三方服務。

The Services may use or link to third-party services, such as advertising services, analytics and crash reporting services, purchase management services, the App Store, or external websites and services opened by the user. Third-party services process data according to their respective privacy policies. We take reasonable efforts to use third-party services that provide appropriate data protection measures.

Data stored locally on your device is retained until you delete or reset it within the app, or delete the app. Data processed by third-party services, such as advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or purchase management services, is handled according to those third-party services' own privacy policies and data retention practices.

You can manage camera, microphone, photo library, notification, and tracking permissions in iOS Settings. You can also manage subscriptions, cancel subscriptions, or view purchase history in your App Store account settings. If you have any data deletion or privacy-related request, you may contact us using the contact information below.
