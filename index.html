1. Information We Process

(1) Local Data

App 可能會在您的裝置本地端儲存功能所需資料，例如使用者設定、播放設定、遊戲設定、轉盤內容、計時器設定、主題偏好、購買狀態、影片清單或錄影相關設定。

這些資料主要用於 App 功能運作。我們不會主動將這些本地資料上傳至我們自己的伺服器。

The apps may store data locally on your device, such as user settings, playback settings, game settings, wheel content, timer settings, theme preferences, purchase status, video lists, or recording-related settings. This data is mainly used for app functionality. We do not actively upload this local data to our own servers.

(2) Device Permissions

部分 App 可能會請求相機、麥克風、相簿或通知權限，用於錄製影片、錄製聲音、匯入或儲存影片、匯出使用者產生的內容，或發送本機提醒通知。

除非您主動使用相關功能，否則 App 不會存取、錄製、儲存或分享您的媒體內容。

The apps do not access, record, save, or share your media content unless you actively use the related features.

我們不會使用這些資料直接識別您的真實身分。

Some apps may use third-party services for analytics, crash reporting, or remote configuration. You can manage camera, microphone, photo library, notification, and tracking permissions in iOS Settings. You can also manage subscriptions, cancel subscriptions, or view purchase history in your App Store account settings. If you have any data deletion or privacy-related request, you may contact us using the contact information below.

（4）如何變更或撤回廣告同意

(4) How to Change or Withdraw Advertising Consent

在適用同意規範的地區，App 會在首次啟動時顯示廣告同意訊息。之後若要變更決定，請開啟該 App 的設定頁面，點選底部的「隱私選項」，即可重新開啟同意表單。此入口只會在適用的地區顯示。

On iOS, Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) is a separate mechanism and can be managed under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
