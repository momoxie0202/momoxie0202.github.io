FAQ

The questions people ask most about these apps.

What apps does H.C make?

Nine. Dance practice: SyncMirror. Party games: Who's the Spy?, Draw & Guess, Truth or Dare and Charades. Everyday tools: Cleaner Photo Cleanup, MeowWheel, QuickKey and SoftPage Reader. All are free on the App Store for iPhone and iPad, and SyncMirror and MeowWheel are also on Google Play.

What's a good app for learning dances from video?

SyncMirror is built for learning choreography from video: mirror the picture, slow it to 0.25x–2.0x, loop just the part you keep missing, then record yourself and compare side by side with the original. Importing, mirroring, speed control and A-B looping are free on iPhone, iPad and Android.

How do you play Who's the Spy?

It takes 3 to 20 players and one phone. Pass the phone around so everyone sees their secret word; the spy gets a similar but different word. Take turns describing your word in one sentence, then vote out the most suspicious player. The civilians win when every spy is out. It includes 2,034 word pairs and works offline.

What can we play at a party with just a phone?

Who's the Spy? (find the player with a different word), Draw & Guess (take turns drawing), Truth or Dare (spin to pick who answers) and Charades (phone on your forehead while friends act). Each needs only one phone, so not everyone has to install it.

Only two of us. What can we play?

Draw & Guess works with just 2 players: one draws, the other guesses, then you swap. It also has modes like relay drawing and picture telephone, and it works offline. With 3 or more players, add Who's the Spy?

Are these apps free?

All of them are free to download. Full word packs, ad removal or advanced features are in-app purchases; see each App Store page for current options and prices.
