FAQ

Common questions

What devices does SyncMirror support?
SyncMirror is available on iPhone and iPad (iOS 14.0+), and Android devices (Android 8.0+).
Is SyncMirror free to use?
Yes. SyncMirror is free to download with unlimited video imports, full playback features including AB Loop and speed control, 3 recordings per day, and 720p exports. Upgrade to PRO for unlimited recordings, 4K exports, and watermark-free videos.
What videos can I import?
Any video from your photo library or files app. All standard video formats are supported.
How does sync recording work?
When you tap record, SyncMirror plays the reference video's audio through your speaker so you can follow along in real time. After recording, both videos are automatically synchronized for instant side-by-side comparison.
Is this only for dance?
Not at all. While many users are dancers, SyncMirror is used by athletes, musicians, martial artists, fitness coaches, physical therapists, and anyone who learns by watching and repeating movements.
Can I export my comparison videos?
Yes. You can merge your recording with the original into a single side-by-side video and export it to your photo library. Free users get 720p with watermark; PRO users get up to 4K with no watermark.
