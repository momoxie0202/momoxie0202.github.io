常見問題

已購買之內容未生效

請確認網路連線正常後，於應用程式內點選「恢復購買」。購買紀錄綁定於使用者之 Apple 或 Google 帳戶，更換裝置或重新安裝後均可恢復。如仍無法恢復，請檢附購買收據與本開發者聯繫。

如何取消訂閱

iOS：「設定」→［使用者名稱］→「訂閱項目」。Android：Google Play →「付款和訂閱」。解除安裝應用程式不會取消訂閱。

如何變更或撤回廣告同意

於適用地區，請點選應用程式內之「廣告設定」。iOS 之追蹤授權請於「設定」→「隱私權與安全性」→「追蹤」中管理。

如何刪除資料

本服務之資料儲存於使用者裝置，解除安裝應用程式即一併刪除。如需請求刪除第三方服務所處理之資料，請依隱私權政策第七點與本開發者聯繫。

FAQ

A purchase has not taken effect

Please ensure you are connected to the internet and select "Restore Purchases" within the application. Purchases are linked to your Apple or Google account and can be restored after changing devices or reinstalling. If the issue persists, please contact us with your purchase receipt.

How to cancel a subscription

iOS: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Uninstalling the application does not cancel a subscription.

How to change or withdraw advertising consent

In applicable regions, select "Ad Settings" within the application. Tracking permission on iOS is managed under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How to delete data

Data of the Services is stored on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the application. To request deletion of data processed by third-party services, please contact us as described in Section 7 of the Privacy Policy.
