常見問題

我買了付費版，但廣告還在／功能沒解鎖

請先確認網路連線正常，然後在 App 內點選「恢復購買」。購買紀錄綁定您的 Apple 或 Google 帳號，換手機或重裝 App 後都可以用同一個帳號恢復，不需要重新付費。若仍未解鎖，請來信並附上購買收據。

要怎麼取消訂閱？

iOS：「設定 → [您的名稱] → 訂閱」。Android：Google Play「付款與訂閱」。刪除 App 不會自動取消訂閱。

要怎麼更改 App 的語言？

App 預設跟隨裝置語言，也可以在 App 的設定中另外指定語言。

我想變更或撤回廣告同意（歐洲經濟區、英國、瑞士）

點選 App 內的「廣告設定」即可重新開啟同意表單。這個入口只會在適用同意規範的地區顯示。iOS 的追蹤授權（ATT）請到「設定 → 隱私權與安全性 → 追蹤」管理。

要怎麼刪除我的資料？

這些 App 不需要帳號，資料儲存在您的裝置上，刪除 App 即會一併移除。若您在歐洲經濟區、英國或瑞士，另有法定的資料權利，請見隱私權政策第 8 點。

App 當掉、卡住或畫面顯示不正常

請先完全關閉 App 再重新開啟，並確認 App 與系統都已更新到最新版本。若問題持續，請依上方說明來信。

可以提供建議或要求新功能嗎？

歡迎直接來信。部分 App 的設定頁也有「意見回饋」入口。

FAQ

I paid, but ads still show or features are locked

Make sure you are online, then tap "Restore purchases" in the app. Purchases are tied to your Apple or Google account, so you can restore them on a new device or after reinstalling without paying again. If it still does not unlock, email us with your purchase receipt.

How do I cancel a subscription?

iOS: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. Android: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

How do I change the app's language?

The apps follow your device language by default, and you can also choose a language in the app's settings.

I want to change or withdraw my ad consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland)

Tap "Ad Settings" in the app to reopen the consent form. This entry only appears in regions where consent requirements apply. Apple's tracking permission (ATT) is managed under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

How do I delete my data?

The apps have no accounts and store data on your device; deleting the app removes it. If you are in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have additional statutory rights — see point 8 of the Privacy Policy.

The app crashes, freezes, or displays incorrectly

Force-quit and reopen the app, and make sure both the app and your OS are up to date. If the problem persists, email us with the details above.

Can I suggest a feature?

Yes — please email us. Some apps also have a "Feedback" entry in Settings.
