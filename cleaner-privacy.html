本隱私權政策適用於個人開發者 Momoxie 所開發的 iOS App「Cleaner 相簿清理」（以下稱「本 App」）。

This Privacy Policy applies to the iOS app "Cleaner" (the "App") developed by the individual developer Momoxie.

簡單來說：您的照片與影片只在您的手機上處理，不會上傳。本 App 不需要註冊帳號。為了顯示廣告、了解 App 的使用狀況與修正當機，本 App 使用了 Google（AdMob、Firebase）與 RevenueCat 的服務，這些服務會收集下方列出的裝置與使用資料，但不包含您的照片或影片。

In short: your photos and videos are processed only on your device and are never uploaded. No account is required. To show ads, understand how the App is used, and fix crashes, the App uses services from Google (AdMob, Firebase) and RevenueCat. These services collect the device and usage data listed below, which never includes your photos or videos.

1. 照片與影片

1. Photos and Videos

本 App 需要相簿權限，才能讓您瀏覽並清理照片與影片。取得權限後，本 App 只會在您的裝置上：

  • 顯示照片、影片與相簿，並記住您在各相簿看到哪一張
  • 比對照片找出相似照片（例如連拍、重複存檔），所有計算都在手機上完成
  • 依您的操作把照片加入待刪除清單、加入最愛或加入相簿
  • 裁剪照片、修剪或裁切影片：編輯結果另存成一份新的照片或影片，原檔不會被修改
  • 在您確認後刪除照片：刪除一律由 iOS 再跟您確認一次，刪除的照片會先放進系統相簿的「最近刪除」

The App requests photo library access so you can browse and clean up your photos and videos. With that permission, the App only does the following on your device:

  • Displays your photos, videos, and albums, and remembers where you left off in each album
  • Compares photos to find similar ones (such as bursts or duplicates); all computation happens on your device
  • Marks photos for deletion, or adds them to Favorites or albums, as you choose
  • Crops photos and trims or crops videos; edits are saved as a new photo or video and the original is not modified
  • Deletes photos only after you confirm; iOS always asks you to confirm again, and deleted photos are moved to the system "Recently Deleted" album first

本 App 不會將您的照片、影片、相簿內容或任何分析結果上傳到網路，也不會傳送給我們或任何第三方（包括下方列出的服務）。若您的照片存放在 iCloud，iOS 可能會從 iCloud 下載原始檔案以供顯示或編輯，這是 Apple 系統的行為。

The App never uploads your photos, videos, album contents, or any analysis results to the internet, and never sends them to us or any third party (including the services listed below). If your photos are stored in iCloud, iOS may download the originals from iCloud for display or editing; this is handled by Apple's system.

2. 本地資料

2. Local Data

本 App 在您的裝置上儲存運作所需的少量資料，例如待刪除清單、各相簿的瀏覽進度、排序方式、相似照片的敏感度設定、最近使用的相簿，以及是否已購買 Pro。這些資料不會上傳，刪除 App 時會一併刪除。

The App stores a small amount of data on your device to function, such as the list of photos marked for deletion, your progress in each album, sort order, similarity sensitivity, recently used albums, and whether you have purchased Pro. This data is not uploaded and is removed when you delete the App.

3. 第三方服務

3. Third-Party Services

本 App 使用下列服務。它們可能收集裝置識別碼（例如 App 供應商識別碼）、IP 位址推估的大致位置、裝置型號與系統版本、App 使用情形（例如開啟了哪些畫面、廣告互動）與當機紀錄，並依各自的隱私權政策處理：

  • Google AdMob：在免費版顯示廣告，並衡量廣告成效。購買 Pro 後不再顯示廣告。本 App 不會要求追蹤權限（App Tracking Transparency），因此不會存取您的廣告識別碼（IDFA）。Google 隱私權政策
  • Firebase Analytics：以匿名方式統計功能使用情形，用來改善 App。
  • Firebase Crashlytics：App 當機時傳送當機紀錄與裝置資訊，用來修正問題。
  • Firebase Remote Config：下載功能與廣告設定，會傳送國家、語言、時區與系統版本等資訊。
  • RevenueCat：處理 Pro 一次買斷的購買與恢復購買，會收到匿名的使用者代碼與購買紀錄。付款由 Apple 處理，我們與 RevenueCat 都不會取得您的付款資訊。RevenueCat 隱私權政策

The App uses the following services. They may collect device identifiers (such as the identifier for vendor), approximate location derived from IP address, device model and OS version, app usage (such as screens viewed and ad interactions), and crash logs, and process them under their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob: shows ads in the free version and measures ad performance. No ads are shown after purchasing Pro. The App does not request tracking permission (App Tracking Transparency) and therefore does not access your advertising identifier (IDFA). Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase Analytics: anonymous statistics on feature usage, used to improve the App.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: sends crash logs and device information when the App crashes, used to fix problems.
  • Firebase Remote Config: downloads feature and ad settings, sending information such as country, language, time zone, and OS version.
  • RevenueCat: processes the one-time Pro purchase and restores, receiving an anonymous user ID and purchase history. Payment is handled by Apple; neither we nor RevenueCat receive your payment details. RevenueCat Privacy Policy

「意見回饋」會開啟 Google 表單，只有您主動填寫並送出的內容會被傳送。

"Feedback" opens a Google Form; only what you choose to fill in and submit is sent.

4. 我們不會做的事

4. What We Do Not Do

  • 不需要註冊帳號，也不會要求您提供姓名、電子郵件等個人資料
  • 不上傳照片、影片或相簿內容
  • 不要求追蹤權限，不存取廣告識別碼（IDFA）
  • 不會出售您的資料
  • 沒有訂閱，Pro 為一次買斷，不會定期扣款

The App requires no account and asks for no personal information such as your name or email; does not upload photos, videos, or album contents; does not request tracking permission or access the advertising identifier (IDFA); does not sell your data; and has no subscriptions: Pro is a one-time purchase with no recurring charges.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

You can change or revoke the App's photo library access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos. Purchasing Pro stops all ads. Because the App has no accounts, we cannot link the data collected by the services above to you personally; to exercise rights such as access, correction, or deletion, contact us using the details below. If you are located in the EEA, the UK, or Switzerland, you have all rights under the GDPR / UK GDPR, including the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection supervisory authority.
