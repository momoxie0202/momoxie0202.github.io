How to delete duplicate photos with Cleaner

Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days, so mistakes can be undone.

  1. Allow photo access

    Open Cleaner and allow photo access (analysis runs on your phone; nothing is uploaded).

  2. Let it group your photos

    Tap Similar Photos or Duplicate Photos on the home screen and let it group your photos.

  3. Check the suggestions

    Each group comes with suggested deletions checked. Confirm to add them to To Delete.

  4. Delete from To Delete

    Open To Delete, see how much space you'll free, and delete.

What Cleaner does

  • Duplicate and similar photos, groupedBursts, duplicate saves and lightly edited copies are grouped automatically. The sharpest one is suggested as the keeper, and the rest can be marked for deletion in one go.
  • Duplicate and similar videos tooSeveral takes of the same scene are grouped with the best one suggested. Copies of the same video (saved twice, compressed, or saved back from a messaging app) are found as well.
  • Swipe up to deleteGo through your library one photo at a time. Swipe up to mark, tap to undo. Every album remembers where you left off.
  • Biggest files firstSort by file size to find the largest videos. Trim long videos or crop photos right there; edits are saved as copies and the original stays untouched.

iPhone's built-in Duplicates album vs. Cleaner

Since iOS 16, the Photos app has a Duplicates album under Utilities. It merges exact copies, and copies that look the same but differ in resolution or format. If that's all you have, use it first: it's free and needs no extra app.

Pricing

The free version lets you swipe to delete, browse by type and find duplicate and similar photos, with daily limits and ads. Pro is a one-time purchase, not a subscription: it removes ads and daily limits and unlocks photo cropping and video trimming. Use Restore Purchases in the app to get Pro back on a new phone.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cleaner upload my photos?

No. Similar photo analysis, cropping and video editing all happen on your device, and no account is needed. See the Privacy Policy.

What if I delete the wrong photo?

Photos deleted with Cleaner go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that you keep.

Is there an Android version?
