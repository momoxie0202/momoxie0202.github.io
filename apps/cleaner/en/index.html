Cleaner
Duplicate & similar photos and videos, cleaned up

Camera roll full, but can't face deleting photos one by one? Cleaner groups duplicates, bursts and similar photos and videos for you. Swipe up to delete, and review everything once more before it goes.

What Cleaner does

Duplicate and similar photos, grouped

Bursts, duplicate saves and lightly edited copies are grouped automatically. The sharpest one is suggested as the keeper, and the rest can be marked for deletion in one go.

Duplicate and similar videos too

Several takes of the same scene are grouped with the best one suggested. Copies of the same video (saved twice, compressed, or saved back from a messaging app) are found as well.

Swipe up to delete

Go through your library one photo at a time. Swipe up to mark, tap to undo. Every album remembers where you left off.

Biggest files first

Sort by file size to find the largest videos. Trim long videos or crop photos right there; edits are saved as copies and the original stays untouched.

iPhone's built-in Duplicates album vs. Cleaner

Since iOS 16, the Photos app has a Duplicates album under Utilities. It merges exact copies, and copies that look the same but differ in resolution or format. If that's all you have, use it first: it's free and needs no extra app.

How to delete duplicate photos with Cleaner

  1. Open Cleaner and allow photo access (analysis runs on your phone; nothing is uploaded).
  2. Tap Similar Photos or Duplicate Photos on the home screen and let it group your photos.
  3. Each group comes with suggested deletions checked. Confirm to add them to To Delete.
  4. Open To Delete, see how much space you'll free, and delete. Deleted photos stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days.

FAQ

Does Cleaner upload my photos?

No. Similar photo analysis, cropping and video editing all happen on your device, and no account is needed. See the Privacy Policy.

What if I delete the wrong photo?

Photos deleted with Cleaner go to Recently Deleted in the Photos app, where you can recover them for 30 days.

Is Pro a subscription?

No. Pro is a one-time purchase that you keep.
