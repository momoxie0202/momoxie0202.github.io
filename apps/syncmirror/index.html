FAQ

Common questions

How do I mirror a dance video on my phone?
Import the video into SyncMirror and tap the mirror button in the player. The picture flips horizontally, so when the dancer moves to their right you move to your right, like following a studio mirror. Mirroring is free.
How do I slow down and loop one part of a dance?
Set the speed anywhere from 0.25x to 2.0x, then use AB loop to mark the start and end of the section. SyncMirror repeats just that part until you stop it. Both are free.
How can I compare my dance with the original video?
Tap record: SyncMirror plays the reference music while the camera records you. Afterwards both videos play side by side, you can fine-tune the timing offset, and you can merge them into one side-by-side video to save or share.
Is SyncMirror free?
Yes. Importing videos, mirror, speed control and AB loop are free. Recording, comparing and merging include a daily free allowance. PRO removes the daily limits, the watermark and ads.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad with iOS 15.0 or later, and Android phones.
Is it only for K-pop dance?
No. Any dance style works, and people also use it for workouts, yoga, martial arts, golf and rehab exercises — anything you learn by watching and copying.
